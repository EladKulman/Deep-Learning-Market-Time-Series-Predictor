ppt/slides/slide11.xml
How we score
 a forecast
The model outputs 21 quantiles for each of the next five days
Weighted quantile loss: rewards the whole distribution, lower is better
Coverage: the 10 to 90 percent band should contain 80 percent of outcomes
Direction is compared with “always up”, not with 50 percent:
in 2025, always-up scores 57 percent

Con
clusions
No particular news source improved Nasdaq-100 forcasting
Fine-tuning learns the scale of returns, not the meaning of the inputs: it widens the bands
Fast market inputs like the VXN change hurt the pretrained model, and fine-tuning does not fix that
